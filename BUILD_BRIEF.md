## 2. How we differ (explicit — same principles, different work)

- **Format: "THE WORLD IN SIX CITIES" — NOT a newspaper front page** (the
  winners' format), **NOT atlas + 24-hour timeline** (our own 19 Nov entry),
  **NOT a dark wire board** (our own 20 Nov entry). Six city-edition cards, each
  a mini front page for one city — RIYADH, ANTALYA, WARSAW, WELLINGTON,
  VICE CITY (Leonida), ROME — plus a briefs strip, a date-check strip and a
  "still to come" footer. The day is told as six places, not one paper.
- **Time framing: "the day so far".** Built ~03:00 UTC, so the image honestly
  says "as reported by 03:00 UTC" and lists what is still to come today (COP31
  third draft, Las Vegas GP, India batting on) instead of pretending the day
  is over.
- **Every story source-tagged on the image** (Reuters, AP, Health Policy Watch,
  IANS, AppGazer, RNZ Pacific, F1) — a rarity in the top 50.
- **A DATE CHECK strip on the image** naming the big Friday stories we
  deliberately left out (Poland missiles, Akvan's landfall, Zecler verdict,
  Gupta sentence, Denmark plot, AI-chip disclosure) — proof of verification,
  not decoration.
- **Title wording is our own:** counts stories + cities, names the structure;
  no "The World in One Day", no "front page", no colour "edition".
- **Hero stories (breadth across regions and fields):** Riyadh missiles
  (Middle East, conflict), COP31 overtime (Türkiye, climate), Rutte in Warsaw
  (Europe, security), NZ–India day 3 (Oceania/Asia, sport), GTA VI $3bn
  (Americas, culture/tech), Italy rail strike (Europe, labour).
